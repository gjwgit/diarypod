[DiaryPod](https://gjwgit.github.io/diarypod/) allows you to record,
notate, and maintain your record of events. It is like a personal
blogging tool supporting note taking during meetings.  It can also
keep track of upcoming events. You can search your notes and you can
include tags for each note. All data is stored securely and privately
on your personal online data store
([Pod](https://solidproject.org/about)). The app is supported by
[Togaware](https://togaware.com) and implemented by [Graham
Williams](https://togaware.com/Graham.Williams.html) pair coding with
[Claude Code](https://claude.com/product/claude-code) using
[Flutter](https://flutter.dev)'s
[SolidUI](https://github.com/anusii/solidui) package for cross
platform development.

Solid Pods are a new approach to handling your personal data on the
World Wide Web and is the latest innovation from the inventor of the
WWW, Sir Tim Berners-Lee. Obtain a Pod for yourself on any Solid
server and link it to your app.

## Introduction

In old school we kept notes from meetings in a paper notebook that we
kept to ourselves for our own reference. Today we keep them in
electronic files, and often have them collected online.  But keeping
your notes online risks them being scanned or scammed for AI or for
fraud. We need to protect our notes. By storing all of our notes
securely and privately in personal online data stores (PODs) on Solid
servers on the cloud we can be free to collect our personal
information and store it securely while having access to it from any
where on any of our devices.
